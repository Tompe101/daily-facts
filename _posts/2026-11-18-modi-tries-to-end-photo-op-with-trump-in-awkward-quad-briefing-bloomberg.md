## Background & Context
The Quadrilateral Security Dialogue (Quad) comprises India, the United States, Japan, and Australia, functioning as an informal strategic forum. Its primary objective is to enhance cooperation on shared challenges, particularly in the Indo-Pacific region, focusing on security, economic resilience, and maritime rules-based order. Meetings often involve high-profile photo opportunities designed to convey solidarity and underscore the alliance's importance.

Prime Minister Modi and former President Trump have historically shared a complex and often publicly cordial relationship. Their interactions during Trump's presidency, including major rallies and bilateral meetings, were frequently characterized by mutual praise and grand gestures, such as the "Howdy, Modi!" event in Houston. These events aimed to project a strong partnership between India and the United States, crucial for both nations' strategic interests.

Diplomatic interactions at summits, even those perceived as informal, are meticulously planned. Every gesture, statement, and visual cue is carefully considered for its potential impact on international relations and domestic perception. Photo-ops, in particular, are vital for media messaging, presenting a united front and reinforcing alliances. Any deviation from these pre-established norms can be interpreted in various ways, ranging from a candid moment to a deliberate diplomatic signal.
