## TL;DR Summary
*   An internal investigation by the Eiffel Tower management has formally cleared the BAPS Swaminarayan Sanstha delegation.
*   The probe concluded there was "no explicit request" from the BAPS delegation to remove female staff during their visit.
*   This official finding refutes initial allegations regarding discriminatory conduct by the delegation.

## In-Depth Report
An internal investigation launched by SETE (Société d'Exploitation de la tour Eiffel), the company managing the iconic Eiffel Tower, has officially concluded, clearing the BAPS Swaminarayan Sanstha delegation of allegations related to their visit. The comprehensive probe specifically addressed claims that the delegation requested the removal of female staff during their presence at the monument.

The findings, as reported by Hindustan Times citing official sources, unequivocally state that the investigation found "no explicit request" from the BAPS representatives for female employees to be asked to leave or be replaced by male staff. This significant conclusion directly contradicts the initial reports that circulated, which suggested a demand for gender segregation during the high-profile visit.

The investigation involved reviewing surveillance footage, internal communication logs, and interviewing relevant personnel who were present during the delegation's tour. Authorities at the Eiffel Tower meticulously examined all available evidence to ascertain the veracity of the claims, ensuring a thorough and unbiased assessment of the incident.

The initial allegations had drawn considerable attention and sparked a public discourse regarding cultural sensitivity and workplace policies. SETE acted promptly by initiating a probe to address the matter transparently and maintain the integrity of its operations and its staff's working environment.

With the conclusion of the probe, the Eiffel Tower management has effectively closed the matter, affirming that the BAPS delegation did not make any discriminatory requests concerning their female employees. This resolution brings clarity to a situation that had garnered international media scrutiny.

## Background & Context
The controversy originated from an incident reported during a visit by a delegation from the BAPS Swaminarayan Sanstha to the Eiffel Tower in February 2024. Reports began circulating, particularly through French media and social platforms, alleging that the BAPS delegation had requested that female staff be removed from their vicinity during their tour. These allegations quickly gained traction, prompting public discussion and concern over potential gender discrimination and cultural imposition.

The BAPS delegation's visit to Paris was part of a larger European itinerary, following the inauguration of the BAPS Hindu Mandir in Abu Dhabi, which received global attention. Such visits often involve high-level diplomatic and cultural exchanges, and any perceived breach of conduct can quickly escalate into a significant international incident. The Eiffel Tower, being a symbol of France and a globally recognized landmark, further amplified the visibility and sensitivity of the allegations.

In response to the growing public and media interest, SETE, the operating company for the Eiffel Tower, announced an immediate internal investigation. This swift action underscored the seriousness with which the French authorities and the Eiffel Tower management viewed the allegations, emphasizing their commitment to workplace equality and respectful visitor interactions.
