## TL;DR Summary
* External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar vehemently condemned the use of terrorism as a tool of state policy.
* The minister highlighted that such practices are fundamentally incompatible with international norms and peace.
* India reiterated its firm, long-standing position against all forms of terrorism, irrespective of its perpetrators or motivations.

## In-Depth Report

### Jaishankar's Unequivocal Stance on Terrorism
India's External Affairs Minister, Dr. S. Jaishankar, recently delivered a resolute statement, asserting that the "usage of terrorism as instrument of statecraft" is simply unacceptable. This strong diplomatic declaration was made during a significant international forum, underscoring India's unwavering commitment to combating terrorism in all its manifestations. The minister's remarks serve as a critical reminder to the global community regarding the moral and strategic imperative to reject any state-sponsored support for terrorist activities.

### Condemnation of State-Sponsored Tactics
Dr. Jaishankar's statement specifically targets the dangerous practice where nations might employ or implicitly support terrorist groups to achieve geopolitical objectives. This approach not only destabilises regions but also fundamentally undermines the principles of international law and sovereign equality. The phrase "instrument of statecraft" directly challenges the legitimacy of any state considering terrorism as a viable component of its foreign or domestic policy, thereby drawing a clear ethical and legal boundary.

### India's Consistent Position
The External Affairs Minister's comments are consistent with India's long-held position on terrorism, which advocates for a zero-tolerance approach. For decades, India has been a vocal proponent of a comprehensive global convention against international terrorism, highlighting the universal nature of this threat. The reiteration of this stance on a high-profile platform reinforces India's role as a leading voice in the global counter-terrorism discourse.

### Call for Global Unity
This decisive statement also serves as a clarion call for enhanced international cooperation to counter cross-border terrorism. It implicitly urges the global community to identify and hold accountable states that directly or indirectly facilitate, finance, or provide safe havens to terrorist organisations. By labelling such actions as "unacceptable," Dr. Jaishankar seeks to galvanise a unified front against a menace that transcends national borders and endangers collective security.

## Background & Context

India has been a victim of state-sponsored and cross-border terrorism for several decades, particularly emanating from its immediate neighbourhood. This protracted experience has profoundly shaped its foreign policy, placing counter-terrorism at the forefront of its diplomatic engagements. From the Mumbai attacks to various incidents in Jammu and Kashmir, India has consistently presented evidence and appealed to the international community for concerted action against those who orchestrate and support terror.

Throughout various international forums, including the United Nations, India has tirelessly advocated for the adoption of the Comprehensive Convention on International Terrorism (CCIT). First proposed by India in 1996, the CCIT aims to provide a universal legal framework to combat terrorism, including provisions for defining terrorism, extraditing terrorists, and preventing terrorist financing. This persistent diplomatic endeavour underscores India's dedication to establishing a robust international legal regime against terrorism.

The current statement by Dr. Jaishankar therefore builds upon a substantial legacy of Indian diplomatic efforts. It reflects a growing impatience with the perceived inaction or selective condemnation of terrorism by certain global actors, particularly when the 'instrument of statecraft' aspect is considered. This ongoing advocacy seeks to close loopholes that states might exploit to legitimise or deflect blame for their involvement in supporting terrorist entities.

## Why It Matters (Impact Analysis)

Dr. Jaishankar's strong remarks carry significant weight in the arena of international diplomacy. Firstly, they reinforce India's moral authority and leadership in global counter-terrorism efforts, putting pressure on nations that might be perceived as engaging in or tacitly supporting such tactics. This assertive stance helps in shaping international norms and galvanising collective action, making it increasingly difficult for states to evade responsibility for actions related to terrorism.

Secondly, this statement signals a continued assertive and principled approach in India's foreign policy, especially concerning its bilateral and multilateral relations. It sends a clear message to all stakeholders that India will not tolerate the politicisation of terrorism or any attempts to justify its usage. This firmness is crucial for maintaining regional stability and ensuring that international platforms remain focused on a unified, uncompromising response to terrorism.

Finally, the emphasis on "statecraft" highlights a critical dimension of the terrorism challenge that demands greater global attention. By distinguishing state-sponsored terrorism from other forms, Jaishankar's statement aims to foster a more nuanced yet resolute international discourse, potentially leading to stronger enforcement mechanisms and accountability frameworks for state actors. This could ultimately impact international legal frameworks and the enforcement of existing UN Security Council resolutions pertaining to terrorism.

## Key Takeaways
* India maintains an unwavering and categorical rejection of terrorism, particularly when employed by states as a strategic tool.
* The statement serves as a potent diplomatic message, underscoring the need for greater international unity and accountability in countering state-sponsored terrorism.
