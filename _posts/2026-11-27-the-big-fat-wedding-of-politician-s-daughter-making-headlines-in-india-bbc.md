## Background & Context

### The Phenomenon of Grand Indian Weddings
India has a long-standing tradition of elaborate weddings, often considered significant family and social events. For many, particularly affluent families, a wedding serves as a prominent display of social status, cultural heritage, and economic standing. This cultural phenomenon, often dubbed "big fat Indian weddings," is characterized by extensive guest lists, intricate ceremonies, opulent décor, and multi-day celebrations, frequently involving significant financial outlays. This trend is deeply ingrained in societal norms across various communities.

### Historical Precedents and Public Scrutiny
In recent decades, weddings involving political families or prominent business figures have increasingly attracted public and media attention. There have been numerous instances where the perceived extravagance of such events has sparked public debate, particularly concerning the source of wealth, ethical considerations, and the optics for elected representatives. These past incidents have often led to calls for greater transparency or more restrained celebrations from public figures, highlighting a recurring tension between private display and public expectation.

### The Role of Political Class in Public Image
Politicians in India, regardless of their personal wealth, are often held to a particular standard of public conduct. Their actions, including family events, are frequently viewed through the lens of public service and responsibility. Consequently, lavish expenditures on family occasions can sometimes conflict with the image of austerity or public welfare that many politicians aim to project, leading to scrutiny and questions regarding their alignment with the general populace's economic realities.
