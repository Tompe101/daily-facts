## Background & Context

### Genesis of the Namma Metro Project
The Namma Metro project, officially known as the Bengaluru Metro, commenced operations in 2011, making Bengaluru the third Indian city to have an operational metro system. Conceived to address the city's burgeoning population and severe traffic challenges, the project has been rolled out in multiple phases. The comprehensive plan involves establishing a widespread network of elevated and underground corridors designed to cover major arterial routes and connect disparate parts of the expansive metropolitan area.

### Development of the Pink Line
The Pink Line (Reach 6) has been a critical component of Namma Metro's Phase 2 expansion. Its development has involved significant engineering challenges, particularly for its underground sections, which navigate beneath dense urban areas and historical sites. The project has seen various stages of construction, requiring substantial financial investment and technical expertise. The ongoing completion of its different segments marks a progressive stride towards achieving the full operational potential envisioned for this vital corridor.
