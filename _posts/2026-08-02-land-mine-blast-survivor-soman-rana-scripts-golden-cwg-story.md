## Background & Context

### From Battlefield to Sporting Arena
Soman Rana's arduous journey began with a life-altering incident during his tenure with the Indian Army. While serving his nation, he tragically became a victim of a landmine blast, an event that resulted in severe injuries, including the loss of a limb. This catastrophic incident marked a pivotal turning point, transforming his life irrevocably and presenting an unimaginable challenge to his physical and mental fortitude.

The aftermath of the blast involved extensive medical treatment, rehabilitation, and a profound psychological adjustment to his new reality. It was during this period of recovery that Rana discovered the transformative power of sport. Initially, physical activity served as a therapeutic tool, aiding his rehabilitation and restoring a sense of purpose. This initial engagement gradually evolved into a serious pursuit of para-athletics, where he found a new avenue to channel his discipline and competitive spirit.

Through sheer determination and rigorous training, Rana transitioned from a recovering soldier to a formidable para-athlete. He dedicated himself to mastering the techniques required for shot put, adapting his training regimen to suit his physical condition. His early career saw him participating in national and international events, consistently demonstrating his potential and steadily climbing the ranks of para-sports, setting the stage for his eventual Commonwealth Games success.
