### Supreme Court Mandates Karthigai Deepam Lighting and Issues Contempt Notice

The Supreme Court of India has issued a significant directive concerning the observance of the Karthigai Deepam festival, ordering that the traditional lamp be lit at the 'deepathoon' on November 28. This ruling is accompanied by a stern contempt notice, indicating serious judicial displeasure over alleged non-compliance with previous court orders pertaining to the religious observance. The move highlights the judiciary's proactive role in addressing matters of religious practice and public administration.

The 'deepathoon' refers to a prominent lamp post or tower, historically significant for the ceremonial lighting during the Karthigai Deepam festival. The Court's explicit instruction regarding both the date and location underscores its intent to ensure the traditional customs are upheld without deviation. This specific directive aims to resolve any ambiguities or disputes surrounding the ritualistic lighting of the Deepam, ensuring its timely and proper execution.

The contempt notice, a powerful judicial instrument, has been issued against parties implicated in failing to adhere to earlier court mandates. While the specific identities of those served with the notice have not been fully disclosed in the initial reports, it typically targets government officials, temple authorities, or other entities responsible for the implementation of such directives. This action signals the Supreme Court's resolve to uphold the sanctity of its orders and to ensure accountability.

This judicial intervention follows petitions and representations brought before the apex court, likely highlighting delays or challenges in the lighting of the Karthigai Deepam as per established traditions or previous agreements. The order serves as a definitive resolution, aiming to facilitate the peaceful and proper celebration of the festival for devotees across the region.

## Background & Context

The Karthigai Deepam festival holds immense cultural and religious significance, particularly in the southern Indian state of Tamil Nadu. Celebrated on the full moon day of the Tamil month of Karthigai, it involves lighting lamps in homes and public spaces, commemorating the formless nature of Lord Shiva and symbolising the triumph of light over darkness. The lighting of a prominent lamp at a designated 'deepathoon' is a key ceremonial aspect, drawing large congregations of devotees.

Over recent years, disputes or administrative hurdles have occasionally arisen concerning the precise manner or location of lighting the ceremonial lamp at certain historically important sites. These issues often involve interpretations of traditional practices, administrative responsibilities, or local regulatory challenges. Previous judicial pronouncements or public interest litigations may have sought to clarify or enforce the appropriate conduct of these religious observances. The present Supreme Court order likely stems from a petition seeking adherence to such earlier directives or addressing contemporary obstacles to the traditional lighting of the Deepam.
