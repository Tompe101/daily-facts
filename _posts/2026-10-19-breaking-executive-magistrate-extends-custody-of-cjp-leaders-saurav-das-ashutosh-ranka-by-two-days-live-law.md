The decision, reported by Live Law, came following proceedings that sought further detention of the activists for ongoing investigation. This extension is a crucial juncture in the case involving the two prominent members of the civil society organisation.

### Procedural Aspects of Magisterial Orders

An Executive Magistrate’s power to grant custody extensions is a function of the Criminal Procedure Code, enabling them to authorise detention beyond the initial arrest period, often up to a specified limit, for investigative purposes. This process requires a reasoned justification from the prosecuting agency, outlining the necessity of further custodial interrogation or other investigative steps. The defense, in turn, has the opportunity to counter these arguments, citing legal precedents or factual inconsistencies. The current extension granted to Das and Ranka signifies the Magistrate's acceptance of the prosecution's immediate necessity for continued detention.
