Union Minister Kiren Rijiju has dramatically escalated political tensions during the Parliament Monsoon Session 2026 by publicly challenging Congress leader Rahul Gandhi. Rijiju's specific call for a face-to-face debate with Amit Shah highlights the government's desire to push for clarity and resolution on critical issues. The phrasing "Don't run away" adds a sharp, personal dimension to the political challenge, indicating a demand for courage and directness in parliamentary conduct.
