This alleged declaration marks a significant escalation in the war of words between the two nations, albeit from a former head of state.

## Background & Context

The relationship between the United States and Iran has been fraught with tension for decades, marked by periods of intense confrontation and diplomatic deadlock. During Donald Trump's presidency (2017-2021), this animosity reached several critical junctures. Key decisions included the US withdrawal from the Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action (JCPOA), commonly known as the Iran nuclear deal, in 2018, followed by the re-imposition of stringent sanctions aimed at crippling Iran's economy and oil exports.

These actions by the Trump administration were met with strong condemnation from Tehran, which gradually reduced its commitments under the nuclear deal in retaliation. The period also saw several military standoffs, including attacks on oil tankers in the Persian Gulf, the downing of a US drone by Iran, and the US drone strike that killed Iranian General Qassem Soleimani in early 2020. Each incident brought the two nations to the brink of direct military conflict.

Even after Trump's departure from office, his rhetoric regarding Iran continues to resonate. His potential return to the presidency adds another layer of complexity to the already delicate situation, as his past policies and confrontational stance remain a significant factor in shaping future US-Iran relations. The current Biden administration has pursued a different approach, attempting to revive the JCPOA through indirect negotiations, though progress has been slow and challenging.
