## Background & Context
### Evolution of Election Commissioner Appointments
The backdrop to the current protest is rooted in recent legislative changes pertaining to the appointment of Election Commissioners. Historically, Election Commissioners were appointed by the President of India on the recommendation of the Prime Minister. However, a significant legal shift occurred with the enactment of the Chief Election Commissioner and other Election Commissioners (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Term of Office) Act, 2023. This new legislation altered the composition of the selection committee, replacing the Chief Justice of India with a Union Cabinet Minister nominated by the Prime Minister.

### Controversy Surrounding Recent Appointments
This legislative amendment swiftly drew criticism from various quarters, including legal experts, civil society organizations, and opposition political parties. Critics argued that the new structure undermined the independence of the Election Commission by granting the executive branch a dominant role in the appointment process, potentially compromising the neutrality and impartiality of the electoral body. The appointments of Gyanesh Kumar and Sukhbir Singh Sandhu as Election Commissioners in March 2024 were among the first to occur under this revised legal framework, thereby igniting widespread debate and generating significant controversy over the transparency and fairness of the selections.
